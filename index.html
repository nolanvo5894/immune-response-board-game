A co-op game for 1–4 players

Immune Response

You command four immune cells. Find the cancer, destroy it, and stop it before it spreads.

1 · Find

Cancer hides

Every cancer cell starts hidden. The Dendritic Cell scans to reveal them, then carries their antigen to a lymph node to activate the Killer T cell.

2 · Destroy

Pick the right cell

Killer T cells and Macrophages hit revealed cells. The NK cell hunts the hidden ones.

3 · Contain

Stop the spread

Tumors drain into the nearest lymph node first. Clear the nodes, or cancer breaks out across the body.

How a turn works
  1. The active cell gets 4 actions. Click a glowing zone to move along a blood vessel or a lymph vessel.
  2. Cancer cells start hidden. The NK cell kills hidden cells. Scan to reveal cells for the Killer T cell and the Macrophage.
  3. Play cytokine cards any time. They cost no action.
  4. Present in a clean lymph node: the Dendritic Cell activates the Killer T cell for 2 extra actions.
  5. Then the cancer acts. It grows and mutates. Overflowing tumors drain into their lymph node, and an involved node lets cells break out to other regions.

Win when no cancer cell is left in any tissue or lymph node. Lose if breakouts fill the metastasis track, the 40 cancer cells run out, or the tumor deck runs out a third time.

Everyone plays one shared screen. With friends, each person takes one or more cells.
